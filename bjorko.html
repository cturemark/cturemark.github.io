TOURIST MAP OF BIRKA
MAP DESIGN

Tourist map of Birka, a Viking-age town on the island of Björkö in Ekerö Municipality.
The design is based on Ekerö Municipality’s graphic profile, but was produced for
educational purposes at Stockholm University. The map was drawn in OCAD, while
the remaining design and layout were done in InDesign.

Scale 1:10 000
