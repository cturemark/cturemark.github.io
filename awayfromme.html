AWAY FROM ME

Away From Me is a beautiful short film directed by Mónica Hernández Rejón about
about a mother searching for her daughter, a mother transitioning from an ordinary
and almost idyllic life, to the nightmarish and surrealistic experience of watching
her daughter disappear. It is a film about the horror around a child’s disappearance,
but also, about never giving up the hope of finding them.

In this project I got to design the official poster and the presskit for the film.
